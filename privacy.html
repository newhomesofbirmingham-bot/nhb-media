Privacy Policy

NHB ContentMachine is a private, first-party publishing tool operated by Nicholas Santiago (New Homes of Birmingham, eXp Realty, Alabama). It is used only to publish content to, and read engagement on, social media accounts that Nicholas Santiago owns. Effective 30 September 2026.

What the app does

The app connects to the operator's own Instagram and TikTok professional accounts to schedule and publish posts, read comments and messages on those posts, and read follower and post-performance statistics for those accounts.

Data we collect

The app receives access tokens for the operator's own accounts, the content the operator publishes, and public engagement on that content (comments, usernames of commenters, and aggregate statistics). It does not collect data from any other person's account and does not use the login of any third party.

How we use it

To publish the operator's content, to reply to comments on the operator's posts, to send the free resource a commenter asked for, and to measure how posts perform. If a commenter asks for a resource, their public username and request may be recorded so the request can be fulfilled.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored on the operator's own computer. It is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties, except that a contact who voluntarily submits a form on the operator's website is stored in the operator's customer relationship management system to deliver what they requested.

Data deletion

To have any data about you deleted, email santiagonick21@yahoo.com with the username you used. Deletion is completed within 30 days. Disconnecting the app from an account via Instagram or TikTok settings revokes its access immediately.
